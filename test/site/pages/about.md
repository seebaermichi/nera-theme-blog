I am Ada Example. By day I edit technical documentation; this is where the leftover thoughts go.

## What you will find here

Posts of five hundred words or fewer, about once a month. Most are about writing and the tools around it, some about keeping a small website alive without a team.

> If a post needs a table of contents, it is two posts.

## What you will not find

- Comments. Write to me instead: [ada@example.com](mailto:ada@example.com).
- Analytics, cookies or a newsletter pop-up.
- A posting schedule.

Written in Markdown, built with [Nera](https://nera.js.org) and its blog theme. System fonts, no JavaScript.
