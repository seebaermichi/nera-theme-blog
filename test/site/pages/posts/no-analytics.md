Nothing, mostly. The counter told me which post had been shared that morning, and I wrote the next one for the same people.

## What I do instead

When a post reaches someone, they write. That happens a few times a month, and every message says more than a graph did.

## What I lost

A small dopamine button. I do not want it back.
